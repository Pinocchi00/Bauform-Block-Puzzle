A puzzle to play. A poster to reveal.

In Bauform, each completed shape becomes part of your composition. Drag blocks into the outline, choose their positions and watch the colours come together, one shape at a time.

Circles, triangles and rectangles form a poster inspired by the Bauhaus. Deep red, blue, yellow and black meet clean lines in a composition that grows as you play.

TAKE YOUR TIME WITH EACH MOVE
Choose from three pieces. Each placed piece is replaced, and each completed outline makes room for the next shape. Plan around the remaining spaces to keep your composition growing and improve your best score. There is no countdown.

KEEP YOUR COMPOSITION
At the end of a game, see your complete poster, view it full screen and share it as an image through your phone's share menu.

A MOMENT FOR GEOMETRY
• Drag-and-drop touch controls, with tap-to-select placement as an alternative.
• A score and personal best saved on your device.
• Automatic resumption of an unfinished game after closing the app.
• Optional sound and vibration feedback.
• Offline play with no account required.
• Version 1.0 is free, with no ads or in-app purchases.

The game interface is currently in French.
